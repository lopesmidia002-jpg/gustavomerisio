# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 with Design Tokens, Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript, 100% Docker (Nginx Alpine).

## Users

Founders, creative directors, and business owners of premium artisan, boutique, luxury, and creator brands (e.g. ceramic studios, specialty coffee roasters, boutique architecture firms, handcrafted jewelry, custom bicycles) seeking distinct, studio-quality brand identity, bespoke web presence, and high-conversion launch campaigns.

## Product Purpose

Deliver an uncompromising digital storefront and marketing presence for Brasa Studio ("Estúdio criativo de marca, site e campanha"), communicating extreme craft, editorial sophistication, warmth, and reliable weekly delivery ("Do briefing ao ar, sem atalhos").

## Positioning

"Recursos de estúdio grande com atenção de ateliê." Combining the commercial conversion rigor, bespoke code, and technical SEO of top-tier creative studios with the intimacy, authorial art direction, and meticulous tactile craftsmanship of an atelier.

## Operating Context

Brand launches, e-commerce storefronts, editorial portfolio presentations, and ongoing monthly creative design retaining clients across digital, print, packaging, and social channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site running on Nginx in Docker with zero framework bloat.
- High-performance, fully responsive layout (mobile-first architecture expanding smoothly to ultra-wide).
- Accessible interactive components (custom drawer, keyboard-navigable Lightbox, WAI-ARIA accordion FAQ).
- Strict adherence to the Brasa Studio visual identity and operational protocols.

## Brand Commitments

- Palette: Near-black background (`#050505`), deep satin surfaces (`#0B0B0B`, `#101010`), Brasa orange/ember accents (`#FF4B19`, `#FFA07A`).
- Typography: Manrope font family, editorial hierarchy, bold statements paired with restrained descriptions.
- Photography: High-contrast chiaroscuro, warm cinematic tungsten lighting, tangible materials, tactile workbench craftsmanship.
- Voice: Pragmatic, direct, authorial, warm, and confident without corporate buzzwords.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live dockerized web application on port 3000.
- Master desktop and mobile full-page visual references (`Brasa-Studio-·-Estúdio-criativo-de-marca-site-e-campanha-*.png`).
- 52 verified static assets (curated photography, custom vector SVG icon suite, and client case studies for Casa Ocre, Torra Rubi, Lume Arquitetura, Âmbar Joias, Vento Sul Bikes, Grão Norte).

## Product Principles

1. **Atelier Attention, Commercial Power**: Design must convert and generate tangible financial return while looking uncompromisingly artistic.
2. **Method Over Shortcuts**: Weekly visible deliverables, fixed timelines, and no surprises ("Do briefing ao ar, sem atalhos").
3. **Tactile Digital Craft**: Rich satin surfaces, micro-animations, and warm cinematic chiaroscuro that feel crafted by hand, not stamped by a generic template.
4. **Lightweight & Independent**: Fast, accessible vanilla code that clients can manage and scale without heavy third-party lock-ins.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Semantic HTML5 structure (proper heading hierarchy H1 -> H2 -> H3).
- WAI-ARIA compliance for interactive disclosure widgets (FAQ accordion, mobile drawer, lightbox modal).
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` for all transitions and scroll reveal micro-animations.
